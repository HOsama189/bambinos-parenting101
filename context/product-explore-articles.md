# Product Context — Explore parenting articles on the landing page

Product working name: **Bambinos**

This file records product intent for parents exploring articles from the landing page. It does not specify visual design, interaction design, frontend implementation, or backend implementation.

Related earlier context: `context/product.md`.

## Primary user

Parents

## Primary task

Explore parenting articles on the landing page

Parents should be able to find and browse parenting articles starting from the landing page.

## Prototype question

Parents finding helpful articles that answer all their questions and concerns, along with helpful categories to choose from

What this prototype is meant to answer: whether parents can find articles that address their questions and concerns, and whether a set of categories helps them choose what to explore.

## Required content and states

- Parenting related content
- A categories list to ease navigation

In scope for this context:

- Parenting articles as the core content type
- Categories that group articles so parents can choose a topic area
- Content present for exploration
- Absence of articles (nothing to explore yet)
- Absence of categories (no category list available yet)

Out of scope here: how those states look, how they are built, or how they are stored.

## Explicit exclusions

- Any design decisions
- Frontend
- Backend

Do not use this document to choose layout, visual style, components, navigation patterns, tech stack, APIs, data models, or infrastructure.
