# Project Brief — Bambinos

**Working name:** Bambinos  
**Document type:** Project brief  
**Based on:** `context/product-explore-articles.md` (primary), `context/product.md` (earlier, narrower context)  
**Date:** 10 September 2026

This brief states what the project is for, who it serves, and what content must exist. It does not specify visual design, interaction design, frontend, or backend.

---

## 1. Purpose

Help parents explore parenting articles from a landing page, using categories so they can choose a topic area and find articles that address their questions and concerns.

The project is a **content exploration** effort, not a design or engineering specification.

## 2. Primary user

**Parents**

Assumed, not confirmed. No user research is recorded.

## 3. Primary task

Explore parenting articles on the landing page.

Parents should be able to find and browse parenting articles starting from that landing surface.

## 4. Prototype question

Can parents find helpful articles that address their questions and concerns, and do categories help them choose what to explore?

Earlier, narrower question (from `product.md`): does a landing surface of parenting articles support exploration at all?

Treat “articles that answer **all** questions and concerns” as an aspirational question, not a product promise.

## 5. In scope

Content and meaning only:

- Parenting articles as the core content type
- A list of categories that group articles so parents can choose a topic area
- Exploration when articles are available
- The case when there are no articles yet
- The case when there is no category list yet

## 6. Out of scope

Explicitly excluded:

- Any design decisions (layout, visual style, components, navigation patterns)
- Frontend
- Backend
- Tech stack, APIs, data models, and infrastructure
- How empty or populated states look, how they are built, or how they are stored

## 7. Required content

| Need | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Parenting related content | Articles a parent would reasonably use when exploring parenting topics |
| Categories list | Topic groupings that make it easier to choose what to explore |

Specific article titles, category names, and answers are **not** defined. Any later examples are placeholders until real content is supplied.

## 8. Success (product, not implementation)

This brief is successful if it is possible to judge, from content and categories alone, whether a parent could:

1. Start on a landing page of parenting articles
2. Use categories to choose an area
3. Find articles that speak to questions and concerns

It is **not** success to decide how the page looks or how it is built.

## 9. Evidence status

Nothing in this project is **confirmed**. There is no research, usage data, or validation on record.

## 10. Open questions

- Which parenting questions and concerns matter most?
- What category set is actually helpful (names and coverage are unset)?
- What article inventory is real vs placeholder?
- How will we know a parent found something helpful (criteria are unset)?
